Choosing AI tool categories for a small business

Start with the workflow, then evaluate a small number of tool categories across sales, operations, and admin work.

AI writing assistant

Can help draft emails, proposals, and summaries for sales and admin teams.

Meeting summary tools

Can capture action items and decisions for a team to review.

Automation platforms

Can connect systems and reduce repeated copy-and-paste work.

Customer support helpers

Can draft replies and surface knowledge-base material for review.

How to choose

Pick 1-2 workflows with repeatable tasks Run a time-boxed test with representative data Measure time saved per person
